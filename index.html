About Me

I grew up in rural Ethiopia, the child of farmers who wanted me to escape the fields they worked every day. I understand the dignity of farming and the desperation of parents who want their children to have more. This shapes everything I build.

I'm a 4th-year Software Engineering student at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University. I came to code late — not because I lacked interest, but because opportunity never reached my classroom. A single teacher changed my path in grade 3. That experience drives my conviction: one intervention can change everything.

Today I build technology for the Ethiopia I know — where agriculture drives the economy, where 85+ languages wait to be connected, and where millions of students sit in overcrowded classrooms waiting for someone to notice them.
